The Science

Clinically proven results

Float therapy is backed by decades of research across neuroscience, sports medicine, and psychiatry.

Cortisol Reduction

Studies show a single 60-minute session reduces cortisol levels by up to 22%, providing immediate, measurable stress relief that lasts up to five days post-float.

Theta Brainwave States

Floaters regularly achieve theta brainwave activity — the state between waking and sleep associated with peak creativity, deep insight, and accelerated learning.

Before You Arrive

Perfect your preparation

A little preparation makes a great float extraordinary.

1

Avoid caffeine for 4 hours

Caffeine can make it harder to settle into deep relaxation. Herbal tea is ideal in the hours before your session.

2

Don't shave or wax

Avoid shaving 4–6 hours before your float. Fresh cuts can sting in the high-salt water — even microscopic ones.

3

Eat a light meal

Float on a comfortable, not-too-full stomach. A light meal 60–90 minutes before is ideal so digestion doesn't distract you.

4

Arrive 15 minutes early

This lets you check in, shower, and settle without rushing. Your relaxation begins the moment you walk through our doors.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Float therapy is safe for most people. However, we do not recommend it if you have open wounds, severe skin conditions, epilepsy, or are in the first trimester of pregnancy. Please consult your doctor if you have concerns.

Everything is provided — towels, earplugs, robes, and premium shower products. Just bring yourself. Swimsuits are optional; most guests float without clothing for maximum comfort.

Our pods are spacious — about the size of a small car interior — and the lid is always under your control. You can float with the pod partially open or with soft ambient light on. Many guests with claustrophobia find they adapt within the first few minutes.

All 200 gallons of float water is filtered through a multi-stage system between every session using UV sterilisation and H2O2. The high salt concentration (25% Epsom salt) also creates an inhospitable environment for bacteria and pathogens.

Many guests feel notable benefits after their very first session. However, like meditation, flotation deepens with practice. Most guests report transformative results after their third session, which is why we offer our introductory 3-session package.
