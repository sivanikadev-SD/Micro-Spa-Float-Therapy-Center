Float Therapy Health & Safety Waiver

Serene Float Micro-Spa · Version 3.1 · Effective Jan 2026
Health Conditions Disclosure

Float therapy is generally safe for most individuals. However, you should consult your physician before floating if you have epilepsy, serious mental illness, low blood pressure, kidney disease, open wounds, skin infections, or recent surgery. Pregnant women should consult their OB/GYN prior to floating. Individuals under the influence of alcohol or drugs may not participate.


The float tank contains approximately 1,000 lbs of pharmaceutical-grade magnesium sulfate (Epsom salt) dissolved in 10 inches of water heated to approximately 93.5°F (34.2°C). The high salt concentration ensures buoyancy — you will float automatically without effort. The water is filtered, treated with UV light, and tested continuously to maintain hygiene standards.


By signing this waiver, you acknowledge that Serene Float LLC is not responsible for any adverse reactions that may occur during or after your float session. You agree to inform our staff of any relevant medical conditions prior to your session.
