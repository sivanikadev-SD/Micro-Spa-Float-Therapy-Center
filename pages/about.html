Our Story

Born from a deep belief in stillness

Serene Float was founded in 2019 by two wellness practitioners who experienced firsthand the transformative power of sensory deprivation therapy.

Our Mission

Healing through silence

We believe modern life demands an antidote. The constant stimulation of our digital world depletes the nervous system, and true recovery requires genuine stillness.

Serene Float was conceived as that antidote — a sanctuary where science-backed sensory therapy meets mindful design. Every element of our space, from the acoustics to the lighting, is calibrated for your deepest relaxation.

Core Values

What guides us

Intentional Stillness

We protect your peace. Every design decision — from our lighting to our scheduling and the overall experience — ensures your session is never rushed or interrupted.

Science-Backed Healing

All our services are grounded in research. Float therapy's benefits for stress, pain, and cognition are documented across hundreds of clinical studies.

Inclusive Wellness

Healing is for everyone. We offer sliding-scale memberships, accessibility-designed suites, and a welcoming environment for all bodies and backgrounds.

Our Facilities

Designed for stillness

Our 4,000 sq ft sanctuary was architecturally designed to eliminate sensory input from the outside world.

  • Acoustic Isolation: Multi-layer soundproofing ensures complete silence in all suite areas.
  • Lighting Design: Circadian-calibrated ambient lighting mimics natural twilight.
  • Air Quality: Hospital-grade HEPA filtration and continuous fresh air cycling.
  • The Lounge: A dedicated decompression space with organic herbal teas and journaling stations.
Ethics & Impact

Rooted in sustainability

Healing yourself shouldn't come at the cost of the earth.

Ethical Sourcing

Our USP-grade Epsom salt is sustainably sourced and certified heavy-metal free. Our essential oils are 100% organic and fair-trade.

Water Conservation

Our advanced 4-stage filtration system (1 micron filter, UV, Ozone, and H2O2) allows us to maintain pristine water while minimizing waste and drainage.

Zero-Waste Amenities

All shower products are provided in refillable glass dispensers. We use biodegradable cups, earplugs, and cleaning supplies throughout the sanctuary.

Our Journey

How Serene Float came to be

From a small studio to Portland's premier sensory sanctuary.

2019 — The First Float

Co-founders Aria and Daniel discover float therapy while recovering from burnout. Within months they decide to bring this healing modality to Portland.

2020 — Doors Open

Serene Float opens with two float suites and one infrared sauna. Within six months, every weekend session is fully booked a month in advance.

2022 — Expansion

The Himalayan salt room and four additional float suites open. The halotherapy program wins "Best New Wellness Service" from Portland Living Magazine.

2024 — 5,000 Sessions

We celebrate our 5,000th completed session and launch the Sanctuary Membership, making regular float therapy accessible to our most dedicated community members.

2026 — Today

800+ active members, 4.9★ average rating, and a team of 12 certified wellness practitioners — all united by one mission: helping you find stillness.
