The Process

Your float journey

From arrival to transformation — a seamless, calming experience at every step.

1

Arrive & Prepare

Arrive 15 minutes early. Shower in our private suites, place earplugs, and leave the world behind. A gentle shower prepares your skin for the salt water.

2

Enter the Pod

Step into your private float pod. Water heated to skin temperature makes you weightless. Soft music guides you into stillness within minutes.

3

Float & Release

Let go completely. The buoyancy of Epsom salt water suspends you effortlessly, relieving tension, quieting the mind, and entering theta brainwave states.

4

Emerge Renewed

Music gently returns. Shower off, enjoy herbal tea in our lounge, and journal your experience. Most guests feel the calm for days after.
